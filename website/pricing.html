Frequently asked questions

Why is EchoKit Pro free right now?

We're transitioning to LemonSqueezy as our payment provider (Merchant of Record with global tax handling). During this transition period, all Pro features are unlocked for everyone. Once the payment integration is complete, we'll announce pricing details and grandfather existing active users.

How long will Pro features stay free?

The free access period will last until our LemonSqueezy merchant verification and payment integration is complete. We'll provide advance notice before any changes. Install from the Chrome Web Store now to get started!

What Pro features are currently available?

All Pro features are fully enabled: unlimited recordings, GitHub Gist sync, advanced URL/body matching, response transforms, global request headers, URL rewrite rules, waterfall timeline view, OpenAPI import, and more. No limits, no trial period — everything just works.

Will I need to pay when the integration is complete?

Pricing will be announced before any changes take effect. We're committed to keeping a generous free tier and fair pricing for Pro features. Current users will receive advance notification and special consideration.

Does the extension collect any personal data?

No. EchoKit runs entirely locally in your browser. All recordings stay in your browser's IndexedDB. We don't track, collect, or transmit your API interactions. See our Privacy Policy for details.
